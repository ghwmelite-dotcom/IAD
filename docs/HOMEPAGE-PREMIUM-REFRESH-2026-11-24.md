# Homepage design contract

24 November 2026. Scope: welcome, services, news/events, digital tools, unit directory, closing actions and shared footer. Preserve the accepted hero and statistics panel, original approved departmental scope and prototype status.

Palette uses existing semantic tokens: white #FFFFFF, canvas #F5F7F8, ink #24313A, forest #205B32, gold #8B691F, charcoal #1C252B. Playfair Display remains the display face; DM Sans handles labels, navigation and controls. Existing body typography is retained.

Layout: portrait-led welcome with text left and the Director right; grouped service links; archive-led editorial news and dated event list; a single charcoal digital-tools panel; compact MDA list; one closing panel and a shorter unified footer. Mobile stacks the editorial layouts and retains generous tap areas.

Critique before build: repeated colourful icon cards, step-number watermarks and per-card entrance cascades had made unrelated sections feel interchangeable. Replace those with hierarchy specific to the content and always-visible actions. Keep the page mostly white/canvas, with forest used for actions and a few emphasis points. Keep subtle motion with reduced-motion alternatives.
